## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Total time:** 30 minutes
- **Prep:** 15 minutes
- **Cooking:** 10–15 minutes (check the package instructions)
- **Tools:** pot, coarse grater, knife, spoon, large bowl

## Ingredients

<IngredientsPhoto src="/images/ricette/quinoa-avocado-e-carote2.webp" alt="Pink bowl with quinoa and bulgur, grated carrot, shredded iceberg lettuce and diced avocado with red onion, still separated before mixing" width={1122} height={1402}>

- **Quinoa and bulgur mix:** 120g (4.2 oz), raw weight
- **Avocado:** 1 large or 2 small (ripe but still firm)
- **Carrot:** 1 large (about 100g/3.5 oz)
- **Red onion:** ⅓ of a medium onion
- **Iceberg lettuce:** 3 leaves
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 2 tablespoons
- **Salt:** ¼ teaspoon
- (Optional) **Fresh mint:** 6 leaves
- (Optional) **Lemon juice:** ½ teaspoon (only if you're saving half the salad for the next day)

</IngredientsPhoto>

## Method

1. **Prep the onion:** thinly slice ⅓ of a medium red onion and soak it in a bowl of cold water for 10 minutes — this mellows its sharp bite while keeping it crunchy.

2. **Cook the quinoa and bulgur:** bring the amount of water listed on the package to a boil, add the 120g (4.2 oz) of quinoa and bulgur, and cook for the time indicated, usually 10–15 minutes. If any water is left, drain it, then transfer the mix to a large bowl, fluff it with a fork, and let it cool completely.

3. **Prep the carrot and iceberg:** peel 1 large carrot and grate it with a coarse grater, so you get shreds thick enough to stay crunchy. Wash and thoroughly dry 3 iceberg lettuce leaves, then cut them into thin strips — drying them well matters, or they'll release water into the salad.

4. **Drain the onion:** after the 10 minutes are up, drain the red onion slices and pat them dry with paper towels.

5. **Prep the avocado:** cut the avocado in half and remove the pit.
    - With the tip of a knife, score a grid into the flesh of each half, without piercing the skin, to get lots of little cubes.
    - Scoop out all the flesh with a spoon, starting from the sides, so you get it all out cleanly from the skin.

6. **Assemble the salad:** once the quinoa and bulgur are completely cold, add the grated carrot, red onion, avocado, iceberg strips and, if you like, 6 mint leaves torn by hand. Mix gently so you don't mash the avocado.

7. <Figure src="/images/ricette/quinoa-avocado-e-carote3.webp" alt="Close-up of quinoa and bulgur salad mixed with carrot, avocado and iceberg lettuce in a pink bowl" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Dress it:** if you're saving part for the next day, first set aside half the salad, drizzle it with ½ teaspoon of lemon juice so the avocado doesn't brown, and put it in the fridge. You'll dress it the next day with 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil and ⅛ teaspoon of salt.

    For the salad you're eating today, add extra virgin olive oil and salt: 2 tablespoons of oil and ¼ teaspoon of salt if you're eating all of it, or 1 tablespoon of oil and ⅛ teaspoon of salt if you've set half aside. Give it one last gentle toss and serve.

## Note

It's important to let the quinoa and bulgur cool completely before adding the vegetables.
