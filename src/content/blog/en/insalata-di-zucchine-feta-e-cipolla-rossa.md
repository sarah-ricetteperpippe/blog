A quick salad that's pretty, tasty, and perfect for using up what's in your fridge. A must for feta lovers!

## Quick info

- **Servings:** 1 person
- **Total time:** about 15 minutes
- **Cooking:** –
- **Tools:** knife, mandoline or vegetable peeler, mixing bowl, small bowl

## Ingredients

- 1 medium to large zucchini, about 200 g (7.1 oz)
- 1/2 block of feta
- 1/4 red onion, about 25 g (0.9 oz) or to taste
- 10 small mint leaves (or basil)
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- A scant 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Method

1. **Soak the onion.** Thinly slice 1/4 red onion and place it in a small bowl of cold water. Leave it to soak while you prepare the rest of the salad.

2. **Prep the zucchini.** Wash 1 medium to large zucchini, about 200 g (7.1 oz), and slice it thinly **lengthwise**, using a **mandoline** or a **vegetable peeler**. You'll have to make do with a knife for the last few slices: they won't all come out perfect! If the zucchini is thick, cut the ribbons in half lengthwise: you'll end up twirling them like spaghetti.

3. **Prep the herbs.** Roughly chop 10 small mint or basil leaves.

4. **Drain the onion.** Drain it well, pat it dry, and roughly chop it.

5. **Assemble the salad.** Put the zucchini ribbons in a bowl, add the drained onion and herbs, then crumble 1/2 block of feta over the top.

6. **Dress.** Add 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil and a scant 1/4 teaspoon salt. Toss gently to coat everything without breaking up the zucchini ribbons too much.

7. **Let it rest.** Let the salad sit for 5–10 minutes before serving.

Serve with crostini or fresh bread!
