## Quick info

- **Servings:** 6 small slices
- **Prep:** 10 minutes
- **Baking:** 40 minutes
- **Tools:** bowls, electric mixer, spatula, sieve, 18 cm (7.1 in) cake pan, parchment paper
- **Heads-up:** whip the egg whites until they hold stiff peaks and fold them in gently, from the bottom up, so they don't deflate

## Ingredients

- 2 egg whites
- 1 pinch of salt
- 90g (3.2 oz) sugar
- 120g (4.2 oz) all-purpose flour
- ½ packet of baking powder
- 20g (0.7 oz) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 175g (6.2 oz) plain yogurt
- 50g (1.8 oz) sunflower oil
- (Optional) a handful of chocolate chips or hazelnut flakes
- powdered sugar to decorate

## Method

1. **Whip the egg whites.** Put the 2 egg whites in a large bowl with a pinch of salt and beat with an electric mixer until they hold stiff peaks.
2. **Prep the dry ingredients.** In another bowl, sift the flour with the baking powder. Add the sugar and cocoa, then mix well.
3. **Add the wet ingredients.** Pour in the sunflower oil and yogurt. Mix with a spatula until smooth and creamy.
4. **Fold in the egg whites.** Add the whipped egg whites a little at a time, folding from the bottom up to keep the batter airy. If you're using chocolate chips, add them now.
5. **Bake.** Pour the batter into an 18 cm (7.1 in) pan lined with parchment paper. Bake in a preheated static oven at 180°C (356°F) for 40 minutes. Toothpick test: it should come out dry.
6. **Cool and serve.** Let the cake cool in the pan, then turn it out onto a plate.

## Notes

Keeps for 2–3 days under a cake dome or in an airtight container. You can swap the yogurt for ricotta or mascarpone for a richer flavor.
