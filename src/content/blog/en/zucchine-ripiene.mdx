## Quick facts

- **Servings**: 2
- **Total time**: about 1 hour
- **Prep time**: 20 minutes
- **Cooking time**: 40 minutes
- **Equipment**: frying pan, baking dish

## Ingredients

* 2 large zucchini
* 1/2 small to medium onion
* 1/2 large carrot
* 1/2 stalk of celery
* 75g (2.6 oz) tempeh
* 50g (1.8 oz) vegan mozzarella
* 1.5 tablespoons white wine
* 1 sprig of rosemary
* 1/2 clove of garlic, core removed
* 1/5 teaspoon of flour
* 4 tablespoons water (2 to dissolve the flour, 2 to add if the filling dries out)
* 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus 1 teaspoon to grease the dish
* ½ teaspoon fine salt (a generous ¼ for the filling, a scant ¼ inside the zucchini)
* 4 turns of the pepper mill
* (Optional) 2 slices of bread, to serve on the side

## Method

1. Bring a saucepan of salted water to a boil and blanch the 2 whole zucchini for 5 minutes. Drain them and leave them to cool enough to handle.
2. Cut the zucchini in half lengthwise and scoop out the flesh gently, leaving enough flesh around the edges so they retain their shape. Roughly chop the scooped-out flesh and set it aside.
3. Finely chop half an onion, half a carrot and half a stalk of celery. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a frying pan with half a clove of garlic (core removed) and 1 sprig of rosemary, then add the chopped vegetables and cook over medium-low heat until softened, taking care not to let them brown too much.
4. Add the zucchini flesh and 75g (2.6 oz) of crumbled tempeh. Add a generous ¼ teaspoon of salt and 4 turns of the pepper mill and leave to cook for a few minutes to allow the flavors to develop.
5. Deglaze with 1.5 tablespoons of wine and allow the alcohol to evaporate.
6. Dissolve 1/5 teaspoon of flour in 2 tablespoons of water and add it to the pan. Stir and continue cooking until the filling has thickened but is still soft, adding 1 tablespoon of water at a time if it starts to dry out too much.
7. Remove the garlic and the sprig of rosemary. Add 50g (1.8 oz) of vegan mozzarella and mix the filling.
8. Arrange the zucchini in a baking dish greased with 1 teaspoon of oil, season the insides with a scant ¼ teaspoon of salt and fill the hollows with the mixture.
9. Cover the baking dish and bake in the oven at 180°C (356°F) for 20 minutes.
10. Remove the lid and continue baking for another 20 minutes at 180°C (356°F), until the zucchini are tender and the filling is lightly browned on top.
