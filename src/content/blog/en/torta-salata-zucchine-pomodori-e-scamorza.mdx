## Quick info

- **Baking:** 40 minutes (45 for the vegan version)
- **Resting:** 10 minutes
- **Tools:** grater, large bowl, 26 cm (10.2 in) pie pan; for the vegan version also a small bowl, a whisk and a clean kitchen towel

## Ingredients

<IngredientsPhoto src="/images/ricette/torta-salata-zucchine-pomodori-e-scamorza2.webp" alt="Zucchini and cherry tomato savory pie fresh out of the oven in the pan" width={1122} height={1402}>

- **Round puff pastry sheet:** 1 roll (for the vegan version, go butter-free — check the label)
- **Zucchini:** 300g (10.6 oz)
- **Cherry tomatoes:** 300g (10.6 oz)
- **Scamorza cheese:** 200g (7.1 oz), diced (vegan version: 150g/5.3 oz of [Affumicata bio](https://www.google.com/search?q=Affumicata+bio), diced — you need less of it, since plant-based cheeses get creamier as they cook)
- **Egg:** 1 (I recommend something like [Uovo Perfetto](https://uovoperfetto.it/), to do our best)\
    **OR**\
    **Chickpea flour mixture** (great for holding its shape when sliced — it sets like egg, and the starch soaks up the liquid from the vegetables):
    - **Chickpea flour:** 30g (1 oz)
    - **Water:** 30–35ml (about 1 fl oz; almost a 1:1 ratio — the mixture should be a nice thick cream, not runny)
    - **Cornstarch (or potato starch):** 1 heaped teaspoon (7g)
    - **Plant-based grated topping:** 1 tablespoon (I use [Grattugiato Vemondo](https://www.google.com/search?q=grattugiato+Vemondo))
    - **Nutritional yeast flakes:** 1 tablespoon
- **Basil:** 5 leaves
- **Fine salt:** 1 level teaspoon
- **Pepper:** 6 turns of the pepper mill

</IngredientsPhoto>

## Method

1. **If you're using the chickpea flour mixture, start here:** in a small bowl, whisk together 30g (1 oz) of chickpea flour, 30–35ml (about 1 fl oz) of water and 1 heaped teaspoon (7g) of cornstarch or potato starch, until you get a nice thick, lump-free cream, not a runny batter. Stir in 1 tablespoon of plant-based grated topping and 1 tablespoon of nutritional yeast flakes, mix well and set aside while you prep the vegetables.

2. **Grate the zucchini:** wash the 300g (10.6 oz) of zucchini, trim the ends and grate them into a large bowl. For the vegan version, squeeze them out well in a clean kitchen towel — without the egg, the vegetables' water will make the pastry soggy.

3. **Make the filling:** quarter the 300g (10.6 oz) of cherry tomatoes and add them to the zucchini along with the 200g (7.1 oz) of diced scamorza (or 150g/5.3 oz of Affumicata bio) and the 5 basil leaves, torn.

    Season with 1 level teaspoon of salt and 6 turns of the pepper mill, taste and adjust. Finally, stir in the egg (or the chickpea cream) and mix until everything is evenly combined.

4. <Figure src="/images/ricette/torta-salata-zucchine-pomodori-e-scamorza3.webp" alt="Slice of zucchini, tomato and scamorza savory pie on a plate" side="right" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Fill the pastry:** lay the puff pastry roll, with its parchment paper, into a 26 cm (10.2 in) pie pan and prick the base well all over with a fork. Pour the filling into the pastry shell, level it with the back of a spoon and fold the edges inward.

5. **Bake** in a preheated fan oven at 170°C (338°F) for about 40 minutes (45 for the vegan version), until the top is golden and the filling has set.

6. **Let it cool slightly** for 10 minutes, then slice and serve.
