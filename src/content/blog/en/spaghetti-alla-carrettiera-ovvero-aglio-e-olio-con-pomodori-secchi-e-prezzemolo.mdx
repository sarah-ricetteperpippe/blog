## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Total time:** 25 minutes
- **Tools:** large skillet (at least 28 cm / 11 in), large pot, bowl (for rehydrating the sun-dried tomatoes)

## Ingredients

<IngredientsPhoto src="/images/ricette/spaghetti-alla-carrettiera-ovvero-aglio-e-olio-con-pomodori-secchi-e-prezzemolo2.webp" alt="plate of spaghetti alla carrettiera" width={1086} height={1448}>

- **Spaghetti:** 180g (6.3 oz)
- **Sun-dried tomatoes:** 5 pieces (not oil-packed)
- **Garlic:** 2 large cloves (or 4 medium)
- **Dried chili pepper:** 1
- **Fresh parsley:** 15 leaves
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 6 tablespoons
- **Water for the pasta:** 2L (about 2 quarts)
- **Coarse salt:** 1 heaping tablespoon (for the spaghetti cooking water)

</IngredientsPhoto>

## Method

1. **Put the water on to boil** for the spaghetti: pour 2L (about 2 quarts) of water into a large pot.

2. **Rehydrate the sun-dried tomatoes:** soak the 5 sun-dried tomatoes in a bowl of hot water for about 10 minutes, until softened, then drain them and pat them dry with paper towels to remove the excess water.

3. **Prep the ingredients:** finely chop the rehydrated sun-dried tomatoes, almost down to a mince (it's important not to leave big pieces: they need to spread evenly through the oil and cling well to the spaghetti), and peel the 2 large garlic cloves (or 4 medium). Finely chop the 15 parsley leaves too and set them aside for the end.

4. **Cook the spaghetti:** once the water comes to a boil, season it with 1 heaping tablespoon of coarse salt and drop in the 180g (6.3 oz) of spaghetti. Cook them 1 minute less than the time on the package: they'll finish cooking in the skillet.

5. **Make the sauté base:** pour the 6 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil into the skillet and add the garlic. Break the dried chili pepper with your hands directly over the skillet, letting both the pieces and the seeds fall into the oil, then add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes and let everything sauté over medium heat for about 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally.

    The oil should sizzle gently, without smoking, and the garlic should flavor it without turning dark.

6. **Drain the spaghetti** without shaking off too much water, once they're ready. Before draining, always set aside a cup of the cooking water: you'll need it to bind the sauce, so don't forget it, especially if you're using a colander.

    Then transfer the spaghetti straight into the skillet with tongs, letting some water come along with them, or drain them quickly in the colander without shaking them too much.

7. <Figure src="/images/ricette/spaghetti-alla-carrettiera-ovvero-aglio-e-olio-con-pomodori-secchi-e-prezzemolo3.webp" alt="close up of spaghetti alla carrettiera" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Bind the spaghetti to the sauce:** keep the skillet over medium heat and stir the spaghetti vigorously, tossing them through the sauce. Add the reserved cooking water, 2 tablespoons at a time, continuing to stir for about 1-2 minutes, adding more water only as needed.

    The goal is to emulsify the oil with the starchy water, forming a lightly creamy sauce that coats the spaghetti and evenly distributes the chopped sun-dried tomatoes: there shouldn't be a pool of separated oil left at the bottom.

8. **Finish with the parsley:** turn off the heat, add the chopped parsley you set aside to the spaghetti, then give it one last stir to distribute them evenly, and serve right away.
