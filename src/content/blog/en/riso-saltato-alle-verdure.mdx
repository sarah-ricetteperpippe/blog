## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Total time:** 35 minutes
- **Prep:** 10 minutes
- **Cooking:** 25 minutes
- **Tools:** 11-inch (28 cm) nonstick pan, pot for the rice, small pan for the eggs

<Aside variant="tip" title="Tip">

You can cook the rice a few hours ahead, or even the day before: let it cool, then store it in the fridge in a sealed container. Take it out of the fridge shortly before you start, so it can lose some of its chill. Cold, dry rice is perfect for stir-frying.

</Aside>

## Ingredients

- **Basmati rice:** 160g (5.6 oz)
- **Zucchini:** 150g (5.3 oz)
- **Carrots:** 100g (3.5 oz)
- **Green onion:** 40g (1.4 oz) (30g/1.1 oz for the base, 10g/0.35 oz for garnish)
- (Optional) **Eggs:** 2 (I use [Uovo Perfetto](https://uovoperfetto.it/))
- **Soy sauce:** 3 tablespoons (45 ml)
- **Vegetable oil:** 2 tablespoons (1½ for the vegetables, ½ for the eggs)
- **Water:** for cooking the rice, according to the package instructions

## Method

1. **Cook the rice.** Cook the 160g (5.6 oz) of basmati rice according to the package instructions, being careful not to overcook it. Drain if needed, then spread it out on a large plate to let the steam escape, and let it cool and dry out while you prep the vegetables.

2. **Cut the vegetables.** Cut the 100g (3.5 oz) of carrots and 150g (5.3 oz) of zucchini into flat matchsticks about 5 mm (0.2 in) wide, slicing them lengthwise first and then in half if they're too long.

3. <Figure src="/images/ricette/riso-saltato-alle-verdure2.webp" alt="Riso saltato alle verdure" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Make the base and cook the vegetables.** Heat 1½ tablespoons of vegetable oil in an 11-inch (28 cm) nonstick pan over medium heat. Add 30g (1.1 oz) of chopped green onion and let it soften for about 2 minutes, without letting it brown.

    Add the carrots, stir well to coat them in the oil, and cook over medium-high heat for 4 minutes. Then add the zucchini and keep cooking for another 3–5 minutes, stirring often, until the vegetables soften but still have a bit of crunch.

4. (Optional) **Cook the eggs.** While the vegetables cook, heat ½ tablespoon of vegetable oil in a small pan. Add 2 lightly beaten eggs and scramble them over medium heat, keeping them soft. Set aside.

5. **Season and stir-fry the rice.** Pour the 3 tablespoons (45 ml) of soy sauce over the vegetables and stir for about 30–60 seconds to distribute it evenly, without letting it fully evaporate.

    Add the cooked rice, stir gently to mix it in with the vegetables and coat it in the sauce, then stir-fry over medium-high heat for about 2 minutes, breaking up any clumps. No need to add more soy sauce.

6. If you made the eggs, add them to the rice, stir briefly to distribute them, and turn off the heat. Plate it up, then top each plate with 5g (0.2 oz) of finely sliced raw green onion and serve.
