## Quick info

- **Yield:** 150g (5.3 oz) of pesto (enough for 2 as a pasta sauce)
- **Total time:** about 1 hour 15 minutes
- **Prep:** 15 minutes
- **Cook:** 2 minutes
- **Waiting time:** 10 minutes to rehydrate the tomatoes + 30-60 minutes resting
- **Tools:** small frying pan, mortar and pestle (or a food processor)

## Ingredients

- **Sun-dried tomatoes:** 55g (1.9 oz) (weighed after rehydrating and draining)
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 40g (1.4 oz) (5g/0.2 oz for the tomato paste, 35g/1.2 oz for the pesto)
- **Almonds:** 15g (0.5 oz)
- **Grated cheese:** 12g (0.4 oz) (I use 6g of [Vemondo grated cheese alternative](https://www.google.com/search?q=grattuggiato+Vemondo) + 6g of nutritional yeast)
- **Triple-concentrated tomato paste:** 8g (0.3 oz)
- **Capers:** 4g (0.1 oz) (in vinegar, brine, or salt)
- **Fresh basil:** 3g (0.1 oz)
- **Garlic:** 1g (about ¼ of a small clove)
- **Tomato soaking water:** 12g (0.4 oz)
- **Salt:** a scant ¼ teaspoon, only if needed (the other ingredients are already quite salty)

## Method

1. <Figure src="/images/ricette/pesto-rosso-di-pomodori-secchi-e-mandorle.webp" alt="sun-dried tomato and almond red pesto" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Prep the capers** based on the type you're using:
    - if they're salt-packed, rinse them several times under running water to remove the surface salt, then put them in a glass of water and let them soak while you prep the other ingredients;
    - if they're in vinegar or brine, drain them and set aside.

2. **Rehydrate the sun-dried tomatoes.** If they're very salty, give them a quick rinse under running water, then put them in a bowl with hot water for about 10 minutes — even if they're already soft (this step both softens them further and cuts down on the saltiness).

    Drain them well, saving a bit of the soaking water, weigh out 55g (1.9 oz), and finely chop them by hand, leaving a few small chunks: they shouldn't turn into a paste.

3. **Prep the almonds.** Crush the 15g (0.5 oz) of almonds in a mortar and pestle (or pulse them briefly in a food processor) until you get a fairly fine meal, leaving a few bigger pieces.

4. **Prep the capers and garlic.** Drain the 4g (0.1 oz) of capers, rinsing them one last time if they were salt-packed, and finely chop them together with the 1g of garlic.

5. **Toast the tomato paste.** Put the 8g (0.3 oz) of triple-concentrated tomato paste and 5g (0.2 oz) of EVOO in a small pan, and heat over medium-low for 60-90 seconds, stirring constantly. The paste should darken slightly and smell more fragrant, without burning.

    Let the paste cool completely off the heat before adding it to the other ingredients.

6. **Build the pesto base.** In a bowl, combine the 55g (1.9 oz) of chopped tomatoes, the 15g (0.5 oz) of almonds, the 4g (0.1 oz) of capers, the 1g of garlic, and the cooled tomato paste. Stir and press the mixture firmly against the sides of the bowl with the back of a spoon for 1-2 minutes: this helps release the tomato pulp and bind the ingredients together without using a blender.

    Add the 12g (0.4 oz) of grated cheese and mix until fully incorporated.

7. **Work in the oil.** Gradually add the remaining 35g (1.2 oz) of EVOO, continuing to stir and press the pesto with the spoon. Pour the oil in a few additions, waiting for each to be absorbed before adding more.

8. <Figure src="/images/ricette/pesto-rosso-di-pomodori-secchi-e-mandorle2.webp" alt="crostino with red pesto" side="right" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Work in the water.** Add the 12g (0.4 oz) of soaking water a little at a time, continuing to work the mixture. The pesto should turn soft and spreadable but stay rustic: you should still be able to make out bits of tomato and almond, while the oil, cheese, and tomato paste form a smooth sauce around them.

9. **Add the basil.** Finely chop the 3g (0.1 oz) of fresh basil and stir it into the now-cooled pesto, mixing for about 1 minute to distribute it evenly.

10. **Taste and adjust the salt.** Add a scant ¼ teaspoon only if needed. Sun-dried tomatoes, capers, and cheese are already quite salty, so you'll probably need none at all.

11. **Cover the bowl and let rest.** Let the pesto sit in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, ideally 60, before using it: this lets the flavors meld together.

## Note

The pesto shouldn't be cooked. You can use it straight up to dress pasta, or spread it on bread, crostini, and open-faced sandwiches. For pasta, loosen it with a splash of hot pasta cooking water, tossing it with the pasta off the heat.
