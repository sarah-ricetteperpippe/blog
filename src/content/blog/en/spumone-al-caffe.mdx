## Quick info

- **Servings:** 4 large or 6 medium
- **Total time:** 15 minutes
- **Prep time:** 15 minutes
- **Tools:** electric hand mixer, large bowl (preferably metal, chilled in the fridge: optional), glasses or bowls for serving (for reference, a standard Nutella glass, about 200 ml / 7 fl oz)

<Aside variant="tip" title="Chilled bowl (optional)">

To make whipping easier, pop the metal bowl in the fridge for 15–20 minutes before you start. It's not a must: what matters most is that the cream is very cold.

</Aside>

## Ingredients

- **Heavy cream (cold):** 400 ml (13.5 fl oz), well chilled
- **Powdered sugar:** 60 g (2.1 oz)
- **Instant coffee:** 2 tablespoons
- **Water:** 50 ml (1.7 fl oz)
- **For topping:** 1 scant tablespoon of chocolate chips, or 1 tablespoon of unsweetened cocoa powder

## Method

1. **Make the coffee:** heat 50 ml (1.7 fl oz) of water and dissolve 2 tablespoons of instant coffee in it, stirring until every granule disappears. Let it cool completely before using it.

2. <Figure src="/images/ricette/spumone-al-caffe2.webp" alt="glass of coffee mousse" side="right" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Whip the cream and add the sugar:** pour the 400 ml (13.5 fl oz) of well-chilled heavy cream into the bowl and start whipping it with the electric mixer on medium-low speed. Once the cream starts to thicken, gradually add the 60 g (2.1 oz) of powdered sugar, continuing to whip until you get a creamy texture that's not quite stiff yet.

3. **Fold in the coffee:** pour the now-cold coffee into the cream and keep whipping with the electric mixer until you get a light, fluffy mousse that holds its shape without turning grainy.

4. **Decorate and serve:** divide the mousse among 4 large or 6 medium glasses (or bowls) and top each one with a few chocolate chips (a scant tablespoon), or with a dusting of sifted unsweetened cocoa powder (a scant tablespoon). Serve right away, or keep it in the fridge until you're ready to dig in; if you're using cocoa, dust it on at the last minute so it stays dry.

## Note

**Storage:** keep the mousse in the fridge, covered, for up to 24 hours. Over time, it may lose some of its fluffy texture.
