## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Total time:** 25 minutes
- **Prep:** 10 minutes
- **Cooking:** 15 minutes
- **Tools:** large frying pan, pot for the pasta

## Ingredients

<IngredientsPhoto src="/images/ricette/caserecce-ortolana-zucchine-pomodorini3.webp" alt="Garden-style caserecce with zucchini, cherry tomatoes, taggiasca olives and chili" width={1122} height={1402}>

- **Caserecce:** 160g (5.6 oz)
- **Zucchini:** 1 (200–250g / 7.1–8.8 oz)
- **Cherry tomatoes:** 5
- **Pitted taggiasca olives:** 25g (0.9 oz, about 2 tablespoons)
- **Oregano:** 1 scant teaspoon
- **Fine salt:** 1 scant teaspoon
- **Fresh basil:** 4 leaves
- **Garlic:** 1 clove
- **Dried chili:** 1
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 3 tablespoons
- **Water for the pasta:** 2L (about 2 quarts)

</IngredientsPhoto>

## Method

1. **Cook the pasta.** Pour 2L (about 2 quarts) of water into a pot, cover it and bring it to a boil, then add a scant teaspoon of fine salt and cook the 160g (5.6 oz) of caserecce according to the package instructions.

2. **Start the base.** Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a large frying pan with 1 crushed garlic clove and 1 dried chili. Let it flavor over medium heat for about 1 minute, without letting the garlic burn.

3. **Prep the vegetables.** Wash the zucchini and cut it into strips about 1 cm (0.4 in) wide and 5 mm (0.2 in) thick, then halve the 5 cherry tomatoes and the 25g (0.9 oz) of taggiasca olives.

4. **Cook the zucchini.** Add the zucchini strips and sauté over high heat for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally so they cook evenly.

5. **Add the rest of the seasoning.** Stir in the cherry tomatoes, the taggiasca olives and 1 scant teaspoon of oregano. Mix and keep cooking over medium-high heat.

    After about 3 minutes, turn the tomatoes cut-side down against the bottom of the pan and gently press on the skin to release their juices, then stir to distribute them through the sauce. Keep cooking until the zucchini reaches the texture you like, crunchier or softer, then turn off the heat.

6. <Figure src="/images/ricette/caserecce-ortolana-zucchine-pomodorini2.webp" alt="Garden-style caserecce with zucchini, cherry tomatoes, taggiasca olives and chili" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Add the pasta.** Drain the caserecce, saving a bit of their cooking water, and transfer them to the pan with the sauce.

7. **Toss and finish.** Add the 4 basil leaves, roughly torn, and toss to coat the pasta. If it looks a bit dry, add 2 tablespoons of the cooking water and toss again, until the sauce turns slightly creamy.
