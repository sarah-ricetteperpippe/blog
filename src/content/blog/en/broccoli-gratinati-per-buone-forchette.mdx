## Quick info

- **Servings:** 4 (or 3 generous ones)
- **Total time:** about 25 minutes
- **Tools:** pot, small saucepan, whisk, gratin dish

## Ingredients

<IngredientsPhoto src="/images/ricette/broccoli-gratinati-per-buone-forchette2.webp" alt="Gratin broccoli in a glass baking dish, with pumpkin-themed oven mitts in the background" width={1086} height={1448}>

- **Broccoli:** 1 head, 500g (17.6 oz)
- **Oat milk:** 300ml (10.1 fl oz), unsweetened
- **Flour:** 15g (0.5 oz)
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 2 tablespoons (1 for the béchamel, 1 for greasing the dish)
- **Nutritional yeast:** 2 tablespoons
- **Plant-based grated "cheese":** 2 tablespoons (I use [Vemondo grated cheese alternative](https://www.google.com/search?q=grattuggiato+Vemondo))
- **Breadcrumbs:** 2 tablespoons
- **Salt:** 1 tablespoon of coarse salt for the cooking water, plus a scant ¼ teaspoon of fine salt for the béchamel
- **Nutmeg:** 3 gratings
- **Pepper:** 2 turns of the pepper mill for the béchamel, plus 4 on top

</IngredientsPhoto>

## Method

1. **Bring a pot of salted water to a boil** (1 tablespoon of coarse salt) and preheat your oven to 220°C (428°F), conventional setting (no fan).

2. **Prep the broccoli:** wash the 500g (17.6 oz) head of broccoli and separate the florets. Trim off the lower half of the stem, which is tougher and more fibrous, and cut the upper half into pieces about the same size as the florets.

3. **Cook the broccoli:** drop the florets and stem pieces into the boiling water and cook for 5 minutes.

4. **Make the béchamel:** pour 1 tablespoon of oil and 15g (0.5 oz) of flour into a small saucepan and whisk over low heat for about 1 minute.

    Gradually add the 300ml (10.1 fl oz) of oat milk, whisking constantly to keep lumps from forming. Raise the heat to medium and bring to a simmer, still whisking non-stop.

    Add a scant ¼ teaspoon of salt, 3 gratings of nutmeg, and 2 turns of the pepper mill. Keep it at a gentle simmer for a few minutes, still whisking: the béchamel thickens little by little, so arm yourself with patience.

    It's ready when it's no longer as runny as milk, but a bit thicker: if you dip a spoon in, it should come out with a light coating. Taste and adjust the salt, nutmeg, or pepper if needed.

5. <Figure src="/images/ricette/broccoli-gratinati-per-buone-forchette3.webp" alt="A portion of gratin broccoli on a pink striped plate" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Assemble the gratin dish:** drain the broccoli well, letting it shed any extra water. Grease the dish with 1 tablespoon of oil and spread the broccoli out evenly. Pour the béchamel on top, spreading it over the whole surface.

6. **Finish with the topping:** in a glass, mix 2 tablespoons of nutritional yeast with 2 tablespoons of plant-based grated "cheese," then scatter the mixture over the béchamel. Sprinkle the surface with 2 tablespoons of breadcrumbs and finish with 4 turns of the pepper mill.

7. **Bake under the grill:** switch the oven to the grill (broil) setting and put the dish on the top rack. Cook at 220°C (428°F) for 7 minutes, until you get a golden crust.
