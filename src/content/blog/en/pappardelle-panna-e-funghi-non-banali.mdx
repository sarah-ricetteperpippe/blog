## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Total time:** about 20 minutes
- **Equipment:** large frying pan, hand blender with a beaker

## Ingredients

<IngredientsPhoto src="/images/ricette/pappardelle-panna-e-funghi-non-banali2.webp" alt="Pappardelle with a creamy mushroom sauce, garnished with parsley" width={1086} height={1448}>

- **Pappardelle:** 160 g (choose egg-free pappardelle for a plant-based version)
- **Mushrooms:** 250 g
- **Cream:** 125 ml (I use plant-based cooking cream: [Valsoia](https://www.google.com/search?q=panna+da+cucina+Valsoia) or [Idee Veg Hoplà](https://www.google.com/search?q=panna+da+cucina+idee+veg+hopl%C3%A0))
- **Garlic:** 1 clove
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 3 tablespoons
- **White wine:** 2 tablespoons
- **Parsley:** 3 sprigs
- **Salt:** 3 pinches (2 for the mushrooms, 1 for the sauce; add 1 more pinch after tasting if needed)
- **Nutmeg:** 3 turns of the grinder (plus 1 more turn after tasting if needed)
- **Water and salt:** 2 liters of water and 1 level tablespoon of salt for cooking the pasta

</IngredientsPhoto>

## Method

1. **Put the water on to boil.**
    Pour 2 liters of water into a saucepan, cover with a lid and bring to a boil over high heat.

2. **Prepare the mushrooms.**
    Clean 250 g of mushrooms, trimming any soil-covered ends off the stalks and wiping the mushrooms with a damp cloth. Cut into slices about ½ cm thick.

3. **Flavor the oil.**
    Peel 1 garlic clove, cut it in half lengthwise and remove the shoot from the center with the tip of a knife. Put both halves in the frying pan with 3 tablespoons of oil and heat over medium-low heat for 1–2 minutes. The garlic should sizzle gently without browning.

4. **Cook the mushrooms.**
    Add all the sliced mushrooms and stir to coat them evenly in the oil. Cook uncovered over a medium-high heat for about 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally. If they release any water, wait until it has almost all evaporated before moving on.

5. **Add the wine.**
    Pour in 2 tablespoons of white wine and cook over a medium-high heat for about 1 minute, until the liquid has reduced and the sharp smell of the wine has faded. Add 2 pinches of salt, stir and remove both halves of the garlic. Taste a mushroom to check that it's cooked and seasoned to your liking: if it's still too firm, cook for a few more minutes; if it needs more salt, add 1 small pinch. Turn off the heat when the mushrooms are ready.

6. **Cook the pappardelle.**
    Once the water is boiling, add 1 level tablespoon of salt and 160 g of pappardelle. Cook uncovered for the time given on the packet, adjusting the heat to keep the water boiling.

7. **Prepare the parsley.**
    While the pasta cooks, wash and dry 3 sprigs of parsley. Remove the thickest stalks and finely chop the leaves.

8. **Blend the sauce.**
    Take 5 pieces of cooked mushroom from the pan and put them in the blender beaker. Add 125 ml of cream, 1 pinch of salt, 3 turns of the nutmeg grinder and 1 pinch of the chopped parsley. Blend until smooth. Taste and, if needed, add 1 small pinch of salt or 1 more turn of the nutmeg grinder. Blend briefly and taste again.

9. **Toss the pasta with the mushrooms.**
    Before draining the pasta, save 100 ml of the cooking water. Drain the pappardelle while they're al dente and add them to the pan with the remaining mushrooms. Toss over a medium heat for about 30 seconds.

10. **Add the sauce.**
    Turn off the heat, pour in all the creamy mushroom sauce and toss well. If the pasta is too dry, add the reserved cooking water 1 tablespoon at a time. Use only as much as needed to coat the pasta evenly, without leaving any liquid at the bottom of the pan.

11. **Add the parsley.**
    Stir in the remaining chopped parsley, saving 2 pinches for the garnish.

12. **Plate up.**
    Divide the pasta between two plates. If any mushrooms are left in the pan, scoop them up with a spoon and arrange them in the center of each plate for a neater finish. Sprinkle each plate with 1 pinch of the reserved parsley and serve straight away.
