## Quick info

- **Servings:** 10
- **Prep:** 4.5 hours (20 min for the base, 10 min for the filling, lots of cooling and decorating time)
- **Baking:** 45 minutes
- **Tools:** 2 bowls, electric mixer, 24 cm (9.4 in) springform pan, spatula for spreading the filling, ruler and toothpicks
- **Heads-up:** you can decorate it Christmas-style or however you like!

## Ingredients

**Base:**
- 250g (8.8 oz) all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa
- 200g (7.1 oz) brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 250g (8.8 oz) plain soy yogurt
- 120ml (4.1 fl oz) soy milk
- 120ml (4.1 fl oz) sunflower oil
- 3 teaspoons red gel food coloring
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar

**Filling:**
- 250g (8.8 oz) vegan cream cheese
- 200ml (6.8 fl oz) soy cream (or other plant cream)
- 120g (4.2 oz) powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

**To decorate:**
- Strawberries
- Whipped soy cream (or other plant cream)

</Figure>

## Method

### 1. Make the cake batter

1. **Set up dry and wet in two bowls:** in a large bowl, mix all the dry ingredients except the baking soda (all-purpose flour, cocoa, brown sugar, vanilla extract). In another bowl, mix the wet ingredients: soy yogurt, soy milk, sunflower oil, red food coloring, lemon juice.
2. **Pour the wet into the dry** a little at a time, whisking until smooth.
3. **Wake up the baking soda:** in a small bowl, combine the baking soda with the apple cider vinegar, it will foam up. Pour the foam straight into the batter and stir briefly (don't overmix).

### 2. Bake the cake

1. **Pour the batter into a 24 cm (9.4 in) springform pan**, greased and floured, and bake at 180°C (356°F) for 40–45 minutes, with the pan on the middle rack.
2. **Do the toothpick test:** it should come out dry. Take the cake out and let it cool completely (at least 2 hours).

### 3. Make the filling

1. **Beat the vegan cream cheese** in a bowl with the powdered sugar (add half first and taste) and the vanilla extract.
2. **Whip the cream separately** with electric beaters, then fold it into the cream cheese mixture, working from the bottom up.

### 4. Assemble the Red Velvet

1. **Release the cake from the pan.** If it won't come out, soak a kitchen towel in boiling water and rest the pan on it: the vegan butter (or margarine) melts and the cake comes free! Then trim off the darker crust and keep the scraps aside (you can crumble them for decorating).
2. **Slice the cake into 3 discs:** hold the ruler next to the cake, stick toothpicks in to mark the cutting line and use them as a guide.
3. **Fill the layers:** place the first disc on the serving plate and spread on about 1/5 of the filling (a layer about 1 cm / 0.4 in thick). Top with the second disc, fill the same way, then add the third.
4. **Cover the sides and top with the remaining filling**, using a spatula. If you want a crumb coat, first spread a little cream over the cake, smooth it out and chill for 5 minutes, then cover completely.
5. **Decorate** the sides with red crumbs from the scraps and the top with whipped cream and strawberries.

### 5. Rest

Let the cake rest in the fridge for 1 hour before serving.
