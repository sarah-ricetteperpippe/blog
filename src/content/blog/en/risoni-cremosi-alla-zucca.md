Creamy pumpkin risoni with no fuss: a few ingredients, a smooth pumpkin purée, and dinner's taken care of.

## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Prep:** 10 minutes
- **Cooking:** 20 minutes
- **Tools:** air fryer or oven, saucepan, frying pan, immersion blender, bowl, spatula
- **Tip:** the pumpkin should be soft before blending, and the finished risoni should stay creamy

## Ingredients

- 260 g (9.2 oz) Delica pumpkin, cut into pieces
- 160 g (5.6 oz) risoni (orzo pasta)
- 1 sprig of rosemary or 4 sage leaves
- 2 garlic cloves (whole or crushed)
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil (1 for the pumpkin, 2 for the pan)
- ½ teaspoon fine salt (for the pumpkin; taste at the end and adjust)
- 6 turns of the pepper mill (2 on the pumpkin, 2 per plate)
- to finish: 2 tablespoons grated cheese, or 1 knob of butter (15 g / 0.5 oz), or 2 tablespoons soft cheese (my vegan options: [Vemondo grated cheese alternative](https://www.google.com/search?q=grattuggiato+Vemondo), plant-based butter or margarine, or [Valsoia plant-based spread](https://www.google.com/search?q=lo+spalmabile+Valsoia))

## Method

1. **Cook the pumpkin.** Put 260 g of chopped Delica pumpkin in a bowl with 1 tablespoon of oil, ½ teaspoon of salt and 2 turns of the pepper mill. Toss well. Cook in an air fryer at 180°C (356°F) for 15–18 minutes, or in the oven at 200°C (392°F) for 20–30 minutes. Blend into a smooth purée.
2. **Get the pasta water ready.** Bring a saucepan with 1 L (4.2 cups) of water and 1 level teaspoon of coarse salt to a boil (less than usual, since the pumpkin is already salted).
3. **Flavor the oil.** Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a frying pan with the rosemary or sage and 2 garlic cloves. Let the herbs and garlic flavor the oil, then remove them.
4. **Toast the risoni.** Tip 160 g of risoni into the frying pan and toast gently for 30–40 seconds.
5. **Cook like a risotto.** Add a ladleful of boiling water and continue cooking, adding more water a little at a time, as you would for a risotto.
6. **Add the pumpkin.** When the risoni are almost cooked, stir in the pumpkin purée. Mix well and add a little more water if needed to keep everything creamy. Taste and add a pinch of salt if needed.
7. **Finish.** Turn off the heat, stir in your chosen cheese, butter or margarine and mix until smooth and creamy.
8. **Serve.** Serve right away and, if you like, add 2 turns of the pepper mill per plate.
