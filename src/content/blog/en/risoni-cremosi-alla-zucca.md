Creamy pumpkin risoni with no fuss: a few ingredients, a smooth pumpkin purée, and dinner's sorted.

## Quick info

- **Servings:** 2
- **Prep:** 10 minutes
- **Cooking:** 20 minutes
- **Tools:** air fryer or oven, saucepan, frying pan, hand blender, bowl, spatula
- **Tip:** the pumpkin should be soft before blending, and the finished risoni should stay creamy

## Ingredients

- 260 g (9.2 oz) Delica pumpkin, cut into pieces
- 160 g (5.6 oz) risoni (orzo pasta)
- rosemary or sage, to taste
- 2 garlic cloves (whole or crushed)
- extra virgin olive oil, as needed
- 3 generous pinches of salt
- pepper, to taste
- grated cheese, butter or soft cheese, to finish (my vegan options: [Vemondo grated cheese alternative](https://www.google.com/search?q=grattuggiato+Vemondo), plant-based butter or margarine, or [Valsoia plant-based spread](https://www.google.com/search?q=lo+spalmabile+Valsoia))

## Method

1. **Cook the pumpkin.** Put 260 g of chopped Delica pumpkin in a bowl with a little oil, 3 generous pinches of salt and some pepper. Toss well. Cook in an air fryer at 180°C (356°F) for 15–18 minutes, or in the oven at 200°C (392°F) for 20–30 minutes. Blend into a smooth purée.
2. **Get the pasta water ready.** Bring a saucepan of lightly salted water to the boil, using a little less salt than usual.
3. **Flavour the oil.** Heat a drizzle of oil in a frying pan with rosemary or sage and 2 garlic cloves. Let the herbs and garlic flavour the oil, then remove them.
4. **Toast the risoni.** Tip 160 g of risoni into the frying pan and toast gently for 30–40 seconds.
5. **Cook like a risotto.** Add a ladleful of boiling water and continue cooking, adding more water a little at a time, as you would for a risotto.
6. **Add the pumpkin.** When the risoni are almost cooked, stir in the pumpkin purée. Mix well and add a little more water if needed to keep everything creamy. Taste and add more salt if necessary.
7. **Finish.** Turn off the heat, stir in your chosen cheese, butter or margarine and mix until smooth and creamy.
8. **Serve.** Serve straight away, with a little freshly ground pepper if you like.
