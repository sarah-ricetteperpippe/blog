## Quick info

- **Servings:** 4 skewers (serves 2)
- **Total time:** 30 minutes
- **Prep:** 15 minutes
- **Cook:** 15 minutes
- **Tools:** mandoline, pastry brush, 4 skewer sticks, baking dish, parchment paper

## Ingredients

- **Zucchini:** 2 medium (about 300g (10.6 oz) total)
- **Fresh mint:** 10 medium leaves (about 2cm/0.8 in)
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 3 tablespoons
- **Salt:** ⅓ teaspoon
- **Breadcrumbs:** 3 tablespoons

## Method

1. **Preheat the oven** to 200°C (392°F) conventional (not fan), and in the meantime get the zucchini ready: wash the 2 zucchini, trim the ends, and slice them lengthwise with a mandoline into thin strips about 2mm (0.08 in) thick.

2. **Make the dressing:** wash and dry the 10 medium mint leaves and chop them finely. In a glass, mix 3 tablespoons of oil, ⅓ teaspoon of salt, and half of the chopped mint; set the other half aside.

3. **Dress the zucchini:** lay the strips out on a plate or cutting board without overlapping them, and brush them with the dressing, first on one side and then the other, making sure it's spread over all the strips.

    Sprinkle the top side of the strips with the mint you set aside earlier.

4. **Assemble the skewers:** roll up each strip on itself into a little roll, and thread it onto a skewer stick. Keep going until you've used up all the zucchini, spreading the rolls across the 4 sticks.

5. <Figure src="/images/ricette/spiedini-di-zucchine-sfiziose.webp" alt="plate with seitan scallopine and zucchini skewers" side="left" ratio="portrait" step />

    **Bake:** line a baking dish with parchment paper, arrange the skewers without overlapping them, and sprinkle them with 3 tablespoons of breadcrumbs, spreading it evenly over the surface.

    Bake in the hot oven at 200°C (392°F) for about 15 minutes, until the zucchini is tender and the breadcrumbs are lightly golden. Taste and add more salt if needed.
