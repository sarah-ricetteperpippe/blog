For roast potatoes, it's best to use old, starchy potatoes. They've got more starch: crispy on the outside, soft in the middle. New potatoes work too, but they tend to stay denser.

This is the simple version: you cut, you season, and straight into the oven.


## Quick info

- Servings: 2 generous servings
- Total time: about 1 hour
- Prep: 10 min
- Cook: 50 min
- Tools:
    * baking tray
    * knife
    * spatula or turner

- Notes:
    * Don't overlap the potatoes: they need to sit in a single layer.
    * Better to turn them with a spatula, not a fork, which breaks them up.

## Ingredients

- 600g (21.2 oz) starchy potatoes
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil (2 for seasoning, 2 halfway through cooking)
- 3 tablespoons water
- 2 unpeeled garlic cloves
- 1 sprig rosemary
- salt
- pink peppercorns

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (392°F) conventional (180°C/356°F fan).
2. Wash and dry the 600g (21.2 oz) of potatoes. If the skin looks good, you can leave it on.
3. Cut them into fairly even wedges:
    * medium potatoes → into 4
    * large potatoes → into 6
4. Put the potatoes in the tray and add:
    * 2 tablespoons of oil
    * 2 garlic cloves
    * the rosemary
    * salt
    * lightly crushed pink peppercorns
5. Mix well with your hands so every potato gets coated in oil.
6. Pour 3 tablespoons of water into the tray.
7. Bake in the middle of the oven for about 45 minutes.
8. Now turn the potatoes with a spatula, baste them with the cooking juices, and add the remaining 2 tablespoons of oil.
9. Move the tray to the top of the oven, raise the temperature to 220°C (428°F), and cook for another 10 minutes.
10. Optional — final grill/broil
    If you want extra crispiness, raise the temperature to 250°C (482°F), turn on the broiler (grill), and leave for 5–6 minutes, until deep golden brown.
